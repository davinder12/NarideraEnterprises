About us

Twenty-five years of outfitting classrooms

What began as a single tailoring counter has grown into a full supply partner for schools across the region — uniforms, accessories, and the everyday stationery that carries a school's identity, all under one roof.

Every order is cut, stitched and finished in-house, so sizing stays consistent from the first batch to the last reorder — a detail that matters most when a school is replacing uniforms mid-session.

We work directly with school administrations on specifications, colours and crest details, and keep records on file so re-orders never require starting from scratch.
